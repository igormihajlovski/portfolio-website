project_overview: >-
  Jaeger Immobilienverwaltung is a professional property management company
  based in Germany, specializing in residential property management and
  homeowners' association (WEG) management.


  The objective of this project was to design and develop a completely new
  website that reflects the company's professionalism, strengthens its online
  presence, and provides visitors with a clear and intuitive experience.


  The website was built in Webflow with a CMS-driven architecture, making future
  content management simple and efficient. Particular attention was given to
  responsive design, information architecture, readability, and search engine
  optimization.


  To further enhance the user experience, the project includes Finsweet Table of
  Contents, Reading Time functionality, a visual reading progress indicator, and
  a Heyflow integration for lead generation. The result is a modern, scalable,
  and easy-to-maintain website that supports the client's long-term digital
  growth.
challenge: >-
  The client needed a completely new website that would present the company in a
  professional and trustworthy way while making it easy for potential customers
  to understand the offered services.


  The main challenge was to organize a significant amount of information into a
  clear and intuitive structure without overwhelming visitors. The website also
  had to provide an excellent experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile
  devices while remaining easy to maintain as new content was added over time.


  In addition, the project required improved content readability, a scalable CMS
  structure, and modern user engagement features that would encourage visitors
  to explore longer articles and contact the company.

my_approach: >-
  Instead of focusing only on visual appearance, the project was approached from
  both a business and user experience perspective.


  The website was designed to communicate trust, professionalism, and clarity
  while making navigation simple and intuitive. Every page was structured with a
  clear information hierarchy, ensuring that visitors could quickly find
  relevant information without unnecessary complexity.


  Special attention was given to responsive behaviour, maintainability, CMS
  flexibility, and long-term scalability. Modern user experience enhancements
  such as reading progress, estimated reading time, and structured article
  navigation were implemented to improve engagement and content consumption.
design_process: >-
  The design process started with understanding the client's business, target
  audience, and service structure. The goal was to create a modern and
  professional digital presence that inspires trust while remaining simple and
  intuitive for visitors.


  A clear information architecture was established to organize the company's
  services into logical sections with straightforward navigation. The visual
  design focused on clean layouts, generous whitespace, consistent typography,
  and a restrained color palette that reflects professionalism.


  Every page was designed with responsiveness in mind, ensuring a consistent
  experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile devices. Throughout the process,
  usability and readability remained higher priorities than adding unnecessary
  visual complexity.
development_process: >-
  The website was developed in Webflow using reusable components and a
  CMS-driven structure to simplify future content management and maintenance.


  Performance optimization was considered throughout the development process by
  using optimized assets, semantic HTML structure, and responsive layouts.


  Several advanced features were integrated to improve the user experience,
  including Webflow CMS, Finsweet Attributes for Table of Contents and Reading
  Time, a reading progress indicator, conditional content visibility, and a
  Heyflow lead generation form.


  The final result is a fast, scalable, and easy-to-maintain website that
  provides an excellent browsing experience across all modern devices.
